Case file 001 Solved A therapist in REDACTED was running ads that went nowhere.
Before

She had 30 years of experience and wanted to launch a new coaching service. Her Google Ads were set up by Google's free support team: broad targeting, keywords nobody searched, no tracking. She thought her website was the problem.

What I did
  • Checked what people were actually searching for before spending a dollar, then built everything around one service that had real demand.
  • Rebuilt her ad campaign from scratch around that service.
  • Built a dedicated landing page in her voice, then used heatmaps to watch how real visitors behaved and kept improving it until inquiries started coming in.
After

5new clients in 2 months

tracked through a dedicated form, separate from her referrals

  • Inquiries went from about 2 a month to about 3.5 a month
  • Her practice went from about 40% to 60-70% full (based on her own capacity estimate)
What happened next

She hired me again for a full website rewrite and SEO, and referred a friend.

This started as a free project while I was building my portfolio.
