The Local Demand Report

Dear Business Owner,

Did you know that every month, people in your town type your exact services into Google, and most of them end up calling someone else?

I found this out while helping a local therapist grow her practice. Part of the work was digging into how people in her area were finding businesses like hers, and what they were finding instead. What I learned shaped everything we did next, and she got 5 new clients in 2 months. Her practice went from less than half full to nearly 70%.

That made me realize something. Most local business owners have never seen this information for their own business. So I turned that research into a short report I can prepare for any local business, including yours.

It's called The Local Demand Report, and it comes in two parts.

The first part shows the top 10 searches in your city for what you do, with the estimated number of monthly searches for each one. Next to every search, you'll see where your business shows up, and exactly who shows up above you.

The second part shows the opportunity you're missing: the one service with the most local demand that you're not capturing yet.

You'll also get a one-page summary in plain English: what you're missing and what I'd fix first. It's short enough to read over coffee and clear enough to act on that same afternoon, with or without me.

You can hand it to whoever runs your marketing, use it before your next big marketing decision, or simply keep it to see how your competitors are getting found.

It should be said that I don't need passwords, account access, or anything private. Everything in the report comes from public search data, the kind anyone could find with the right tools and a few hours of digging. Every number is checked by hand and current as of the week I send it.

All I ask is $50, enough to cover the tools and time that go into each one. And if your report doesn't show you at least one thing you didn't already know, tell me and I'll refund every cent.

Because I research every report by hand, I can only take 10 each week. If you'd like yours, let me know soon.

Just send me your business name, city, and main services through the form below. I'll confirm I have the right business before you pay anything, then send your report to your email within 5 business days.

Sincerely,

Omar Aybak

P.S. One report is $50. Have more than one location? Each extra location ordered at the same time is just $25.
