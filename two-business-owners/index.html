The Two Business Owners

Same start. Same skills. One made three times more. Here's why.

Dear Business Owner,

In the bleak midwinter, about a year ago, two business owners opened the same kind of business, at the same time, in the same city.

They were very much alike, these two. Both were skilled at what they did. Both were well liked by their customers. And both, as new business owners usually are, were filled with ambitious dreams of growth.

Recently, the two met again at an industry event.

They were still very much alike. Both offered the same service. Both had a long list of happy clients. Both were talented, and both were loved for it.

But there was a difference. One of them was making three times as much money as the other. And the other had no idea, because by his own measure, business was going great.

What Made The Difference

Have you ever wondered, as I have, what makes this kind of difference between two people? It isn't raw intelligence, talent, or even hard work. It isn't that one wants success and the other doesn't.

The difference lies in the decisions each one made, and how they made them. One decision above all: who they trusted to help them grow.

That is why I am writing to you, and to business owners like you. Because the people you bring into your business will either move it forward or keep it exactly where it is.

A Strategic Marketer Unlike Any Other

You see, the right strategic marketer is rare. Before he ever sends you a message, he has already studied your niche. He knows who your customers are, what makes them buy, and what makes them walk away.

He studies what the top players in your market are doing, which campaigns are working for them, and where the gaps are. And he does not reach out to a single business owner until he has found a specific problem in that business that he knows how to fix. A problem that, once fixed, brings in more clients.

The Right Decision Is Key

Deciding whether to hire a marketer is a big decision. Knowing how to choose the right one is an even bigger one.

A great strategic marketer doesn't guess what you or your customers want. He knows it before the first call. He knows the problem he's going to solve before you've even described it.

Why? Because a good marketer will never waste your time, or his own, pitching a business he isn't sure he can help. Only marketers chasing a quick paycheck do that. The real ones do this work because they enjoy watching a business grow and its owner succeed.

If you have never worked with a strategic marketer like this, you cannot imagine how useful one can be to you.
