Your data, in plain English.

What I collect, why, where it lives, and how to make it disappear. Last updated October 15, 2026.

Who I am

This site is run by Omar Aybak, trading as Omar Results. If you have any question about your data, email me at omaraybak89@gmail.com. You'll get a reply from me, not a support queue.

What I collect

If you request the free guide on the Resources page, I collect your first name, your email address, your answers to the three short questions, the page you were on, and any campaign tags in the link you clicked (like utm_source). If you choose to share your website afterwards, I store that link too.

If you email me, I get whatever you put in the email, like any other inbox.

When you browse, Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights count page views and measure how fast pages load. They don't use cookies and don't identify you personally.

Why I collect it

To send you the guide you asked for, to reply to you, to send the occasional useful email if you signed up for the guide, and to understand which pages are working so I can improve them. That's it.

Where it's stored

Guide requests go into a private Google Sheet that only I can access. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Email lives in Gmail. These companies process the data on my behalf under their own privacy terms.

What I never do

I don't sell your data, rent it, or share it with other marketers. I don't run advertising trackers on this site.

How long I keep it

As long as it's useful for the reason you gave it to me. If we never end up talking, I'll clear old guide requests out periodically. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Your choices

Every email I send has a way to unsubscribe. You can also email me to see what I have about you, correct it, or delete it. I'll do it within a few days and confirm when it's done.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
